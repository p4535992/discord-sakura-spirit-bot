## Spirit Ecosystem

The Spirit Ecosystem is the heart of the Sakura Spirit Bot, featuring AI-driven entities that bring life to your Discord server.

### Spirit Companions

Every user can adopt a spirit companion that grows and evolves alongside them. Spirit companions have unique personalities generated by AI, making each one truly unique.

**Key Features:**

- **Personality System**: Each spirit has one of six personality types: cheerful, mysterious, stoic, playful, wise, or mischievous
- **Evolution Stages**: Spirits evolve through multiple stages as bond level increases
- **Power Scaling**: Spirit power increases with evolution and user level
- **Mood System**: Spirits have dynamic moods that change based on server weather, harmony, and interactions
- **Custom Naming**: Users can give their spirits custom names

**Factions:**

Spirits belong to one of three factions, each with distinct characteristics:

**Blossoms Faction**: Nature-aligned spirits that embody growth, harmony, and renewal. They prefer gentle weather conditions like Petal Storm and Gentle Breeze. Blossoms spirits are often cheerful or wise, focusing on healing and support abilities.

**Moonborn Faction**: Light and shadow spirits that thrive in mystery and contemplation. They are most active during Lunar Bloom and Eclipse weather. Moonborn spirits tend to be mysterious or stoic, with abilities related to illusion and insight.

**Stormkin Faction**: Chaos and power spirits that embrace change and unpredictability. They are energized by Thunder Blossom and Chaos Wind weather. Stormkin spirits are typically mischievous or playful, wielding offensive and disruptive abilities.

### AI-Powered Interactions

Spirits use OpenAI's GPT models to generate contextual, personality-driven responses. The AI considers multiple factors when generating responses:

- Spirit's personality type and current mood
- Current server weather conditions
- User's emotional state
- Recent interaction history
- Spirit's faction alignment

This creates genuinely engaging conversations that feel alive and responsive to the server's atmosphere.

### Spirit Roaming

Spirits can autonomously roam through server channels, appearing randomly to interact with users. The roaming system considers:

- Server activity levels
- Current weather effects on spirit activity
- Spirit faction preferences
- Time-based spawn rates

When a spirit appears, users can interact with it to gain bond points, discover lore, or receive temporary buffs.

### Bond and Evolution System

The bond system tracks the relationship between a user and their spirit companion:

**Bond Levels**: Gained through regular interactions, completing quests together, and participating in server events. Each interaction grants 1-5 bond points.

**Evolution Thresholds**: Spirits evolve when bond level reaches 100 × current evolution stage. For example, evolution to stage 2 requires 100 bond, stage 3 requires 200 bond, and so on.

**Evolution Benefits**: Each evolution increases spirit power by 10, unlocks new abilities, and may change the spirit's appearance description.

## Weather System

The Dynamic Weather System creates an ever-changing atmosphere that affects all aspects of gameplay.

### Weather Types

The bot features twelve distinct weather types, each with unique effects:

**Petal Storm** (Uncommon): Cherry blossoms swirl violently through the air. Effects include 30% increased XP gain, 10% gacha luck boost, and high spirit activity.

**Lunar Bloom** (Rare): Moonlight illuminates the blossoms with ethereal glow. Provides 20% XP boost, enhanced dream quality, and medium spirit activity.

**Eclipse** (Rare): Darkness falls as spirits grow restless. Grants 50% XP boost, doubles event spawn rate, and creates very high spirit activity.

**Mistfall** (Common): Gentle mist obscures the world in mystery. Normal XP rates, 50% increased secret discovery chance, and low spirit activity.

**Crimson Dawn** (Uncommon): The sky burns red at dawn, filling all with energy. Offers 40% XP boost, 30% currency gain increase, and high spirit activity.

**Starlight Shower** (Rare): Stars fall like rain, granting wishes. Provides 30% XP boost, 50% gacha luck increase, and medium spirit activity.

**Gentle Breeze** (Common): Calm wind carries petals softly. Standard XP rates, harmony score boost, and medium spirit activity.

**Thunder Blossom** (Epic): Lightning crackles through cherry trees. Grants 60% XP boost, 50% increased event spawns, and very high spirit activity.

**Frost Petal** (Uncommon): Ice crystals form on blossoms. Offers 10% XP boost, 20% spirit power increase, and low spirit activity.

**Golden Hour** (Common): Warm golden light bathes everything. Provides 20% XP boost, harmony boost, 20% currency increase, and medium spirit activity.

**Twilight Veil** (Uncommon): Day and night blur as magic flows freely. Grants 30% XP boost, 20% gacha luck, 30% secret discovery, and high spirit activity.

**Chaos Wind** (Legendary): Unpredictable winds tear through the realm. Offers 100% XP boost, random events enabled, but 20% reduced gacha luck, with chaotic spirit activity.

### Weather Effects

Weather affects multiple game systems:

**XP Multipliers**: Range from 1.0x (normal) to 2.0x (Chaos Wind), directly affecting experience gain from all activities.

**Gacha Luck**: Influences rarity rates in gacha pulls, with multipliers ranging from 0.8x to 1.5x.

**Currency Gain**: Certain weather types increase currency rewards from activities and gifts.

**Spirit Activity**: Determines how frequently spirits roam and how likely they are to appear in channels.

**Event Spawning**: Some weather types increase the likelihood of special events occurring.

**Harmony Effects**: Specific weather types can boost or drain server harmony scores.

### Weather Rotation

Weather changes occur through multiple mechanisms:

**Scheduled Changes**: Automatic weather rotation every hour based on server harmony and previous weather patterns.

**Ritual-Triggered**: Players can perform rituals to influence weather changes.

**Manual Override**: Server administrators can manually set weather using admin commands.

**Rarity-Based Selection**: Weather types are selected based on rarity weights, with common weather appearing more frequently than legendary weather.

## Social Features

The Social Features system creates meaningful connections between users and enhances community engagement.

### Emotional Engine

The bot tracks each user's emotional state, which influences various gameplay aspects:

**Emotional States**: Users can be in one of eight states: Blossoming (positive growth), Wilting (sadness), Chaotic Bloom (excitement), Lunar Calm (peace), Radiant (joy), Melancholic (contemplation), Energized (activity), or Peaceful (rest).

**State Detection**: The AI analyzes recent messages to determine emotional shifts, considering message tone, frequency, and content.

**Gameplay Impact**: Emotional states affect gacha rates, quest rewards, dream content, and spirit interactions.

### Relationship Tracking

The bot monitors relationships between users across four dimensions:

**Warmth Score**: Increases through positive interactions like gift-giving, cooperative activities, and friendly conversations.

**Rivalry Score**: Grows through competitive activities, playful challenges, and gaming competitions.

**Synergy Score**: Enhanced by successful teamwork, ritual participation, and coordinated gameplay.

**Chaos Score**: Rises through unpredictable interactions, random events, and spontaneous activities.

These scores influence ritual outcomes, boss battle bonuses, and unlock special lore entries.

### Daily Dreams

Every user receives a personalized dream each day, generated based on their emotional state and recent activities:

**Dream Types**: Prophetic (hints at future events), Empowering (grants buffs), Mysterious (unlocks secrets), Peaceful (provides rest bonuses), or Chaotic (random effects).

**Dream Effects**: Can include XP boosts, gacha luck increases, harmony contributions, or special item discoveries.

**Persistence**: Dreams last for 24 hours and can be viewed multiple times, but effects only apply once.

### Gift System

Users can send gifts to each other, strengthening relationships and providing benefits:

**Gift Types**: Flowers (🌸), Stars (⭐), Hearts (💖), Moons (🌙), and Crystals (💎).

**Cost and Rewards**: Sending a gift costs 50 currency, while the recipient receives 30 currency plus relationship score increases.

**Statistics Tracking**: The system tracks total gifts sent and received, contributing to user achievements.

## Gaming Utilities

Gaming Utilities provide practical tools for coordinating gameplay and enhancing the gaming experience.

### LFG System

The Looking for Group system helps players find teammates:

**Post Creation**: Users create LFG posts specifying the game, party size, and optional message.

**Automatic Tracking**: The bot tracks who joins via reactions and updates the post in real-time.

**Party Management**: When the party fills, the post automatically marks as "Full" and changes color.

**Voice Integration**: The bot monitors voice channel activity and can suggest LFG creation when multiple users join.

### Ritual Buffs

Pre-game rituals provide thematic buffs before gaming sessions:

**Victory Ritual**: Grants accuracy, confidence, and speed bonuses focused on winning.

**Focus Ritual**: Enhances concentration, awareness, and reaction time for competitive play.

**Teamwork Ritual**: Boosts team synergy, communication, and support abilities.

**Luck Ritual**: Increases luck, critical chance, and RNG favor for games with random elements.

**Duration**: Buffs last for the next three games or two hours, whichever comes first.

**Cooldown**: Thirty-minute cooldown prevents spam while allowing regular use.

### Statistics Tracking

The bot tracks comprehensive gaming statistics:

- Total games played across all titles
- LFG sessions created and joined
- Rituals performed and their types
- Win rates (when game API integration is available)
- Favorite games and play times

## World Exploration

Sakura Park is a persistent virtual world that users can explore, featuring multiple zones, NPCs, and activities.

### Park Zones

The park consists of eight interconnected zones:

**Park Entrance** (🌸): The grand torii gate entrance with a welcome NPC who guides new visitors.

**Cherry Blossom Garden** (🌺): Endless rows of cherry trees with collectibles and photo spots.

**Koi Pond** (🎣): A serene fishing area with a meditation spot and mini-game.

**Sacred Shrine** (⛩️): An ancient shrine for prayers and spirit encounters.

**Spirit Shop** (🏪): A mystical shop run by a tanuki spirit selling items and cosmetics.

**Moonlit Teahouse** (🍵): A cozy rest area where users can chat with NPCs and recover.

**Mystic Forest** (🌲): A dense forest with rare spirits and hidden secrets.

**Blossom Summit** (⛰️): The highest point offering panoramic views and rare events.

### Exploration Mechanics

**Zone Connections**: Each zone connects to specific other zones, creating a navigable map.

**Discovery System**: Users have a 20% chance to discover hidden items, spirits, or secrets when exploring.

**Rewards**: Discoveries can grant currency, XP, rare items, or unlock new lore entries.

**Progress Tracking**: The bot tracks which zones users have visited and how many times.

### Shop System

The Spirit Shop offers various items for purchase:

- **Spirit Charm** (100 currency): Increases spirit bond gain
- **Lucky Petal** (150 currency): Temporary gacha luck boost
- **XP Potion** (200 currency): Instant 50 XP
- **Weather Charm** (500 currency): Influence weather changes
- **Rare Spirit Egg** (1000 currency): Hatch a rare spirit

### Quest System

Quests provide structured objectives with rewards:

**Quest Types**: Daily (repeatable), Weekly (reset weekly), Story (one-time), and Special (event-limited).

**Difficulty Levels**: Easy, Medium, and Hard quests with scaling rewards.

**Objectives**: Can include exploration, collection, combat, social, or discovery tasks.

**Rewards**: Include XP, currency, items, cosmetics, and badges.

## Gacha System

The Gacha System provides excitement and rewards through randomized pulls.

### Pull Mechanics

**Cost**: Each pull costs 100 currency.

**Rarity Rates**: Common (60%), Uncommon (25%), Rare (10%), Epic (4%), Legendary (1%).

**Weather Influence**: Current weather can modify these rates through luck multipliers.

**Emotional Influence**: User emotional state can slightly affect pull outcomes.

### Reward Types

**Spirits**: New spirit companions with varying rarities and factions.

**Cosmetics**: Auras, role colors, profile backgrounds, and visual effects.

**Items**: Consumables like XP potions, currency boosters, and special charms.

**Badges**: Collectible badges that display on profiles and in the server.

### Inventory System

All pulled items are stored in the user's inventory:

- Organized by item type
- Displays rarity and quantity
- Tracks acquisition date
- Supports equipping cosmetics
- Shows item metadata and effects

## Progression System

The Progression System provides long-term goals and rewards.

### Experience and Leveling

**XP Sources**: Gained from messages (with cooldown), completing quests, exploring zones, and participating in events.

**Level Calculation**: XP required for next level = 100 × (current level)^1.5.

**Weather Multipliers**: Current weather affects XP gain rates.

**Level Benefits**: Higher levels unlock new zones, quests, and features.

### Currency System

**Earning Currency**: Through leveling up, completing quests, selling items, receiving gifts, and random discoveries.

**Spending Currency**: On gacha pulls, shop items, sending gifts, and special services.

**Weather Bonuses**: Some weather types increase currency gain.

### Achievement System

While not fully implemented in this version, the foundation exists for:

- Exploration achievements (visit all zones)
- Social achievements (send 100 gifts)
- Spirit achievements (reach max bond)
- Gacha achievements (pull a legendary)
- Gaming achievements (complete 50 LFG sessions)
